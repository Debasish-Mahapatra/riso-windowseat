# Resonance: optional call-and-response form

An optional story of paired voices and a response; read it only when the user requests it or the
subject deliberately calls for it.

It is not the default: new work starts from [the brief](../brief.md), whose contracts and craft
checks still apply. [Lumen](../../films/lumen/FILM.md) and
[Emergence](../../films/emergence/FILM.md) adapt this form; their endings and score cues are not
templates for other subjects.

## Premise

A lone ink dot calls with ripples. Illustrated worlds answer and become memories. A second dot
answers from outside the frame; their rings meet, a flower opens, and the world becomes a universe
of pairs. A drawn title can close it. This form is the origin of the blue/pink pair, central dot,
iris worlds, memory archive, collision, bloom, two-dot mark and lettered ending: keep or adapt
them consciously; they are not the project's identity.

## Original arrangement

About 28 s on a 120 BPM grid; retime when the concept needs it.

| Seconds | Passage | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 0–1.5 | Call | Central indigo dot on cream sends two blue ripples and small rim ticks. |
| 1.5–4.5 | Windows | Ripples open disc worlds (koi pond, cricket on a moon, jellyfish); paper holds may separate them. |
| 4.5–6 | Dive | Window goes full bleed; owl, bell and lighthouse scenes open over one another. |
| 6–13.9 | Responses | Varied resonating subjects gather pace: beat cuts or overlapping reveals, sparse holds for breath. |
| 13.9–15.9 | Recollection | Earlier scenes return in alternate colourways behind one continuously tightening lens. |
| 15.9–17.8 | Archive | ~40 medallions orbit, drain to blue, shrink and scatter, leaving the lone dot. |
| 17.8–22 | Answer | A pink voice arrives from lower left; alternating ripples approach the blue. |
| 22–23 | Contact | Sparks at the meeting point; a pink lotus with a yellow-orange heart unfolds. |
| 23–24 | Together | Short pink/blue reprise carries both dots through the pictures. |
| 24–26 | Expansion | In an indigo night the pair becomes one of hundreds of orbiting pairs. |
| 26–28 | Signature | Title and optional byline draw on cream with the pair as a two-dot mark, then dissolve. |

Faithful version: the first dot stays at (540, 540) through call, responses and archive, with a
meaningful identity in each scene (spindle, throat, opening, light source, eye); the second dot
enters only in the answer. The mark was ~2.2% of frame width, indigo with a fixed pink
registration fringe. The two colours can have distinct voices, originally a minor third apart;
score per [sound.md](../sound.md), continuous through visual rests, accents on visible contact
and expansion.

Subject pool (not a checklist): wolf under aurora; rotary telephone; turntable; frogs under a
moon; bats leaving a cave; breaking wave; cat at a rainy window; sunflower and bee; valve radio;
fireworks; hummingbird; kettle; Ferris wheel; waterfall; bicycle and bougainvillea; whale; piano
or harp; rocket; apartment windows; ringed planet; balloons; volcano; radio telescope; campfire;
sunflower field; mountain ridges; umbrella pine; rain on a pond.

## Current craft still applies

- Reveal pinned artwork with a moving clip; keep the outgoing world until coverage is complete.
- Use current transition and loop guidance ([motion.md](../motion.md)); don't repeat clearing
  ripples just because the original timing had them.
- Bake medallions at display size; treat the ink drain as a deliberate plate transformation.
- The original prompt's older technical choices are superseded by the current contracts.
