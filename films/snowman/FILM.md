# Snowman

A 70-second linocut film at 1920 × 1080, 24 fps, drawn on twos. It uses two blocks, black and
red, printed on cream paper.

Every winter a boy builds a snowman in front of his house in the Himachal hills. Each year he is a
little bigger and the snowman a little smaller. In 2025 there is no snow at all, so he builds
his daughter one out of the freezer's frost. The last line is "How small it gets is up to us."

## Look

- **The print.**
  - Each plate is an alpha mask: *ink* adds, *cut* removes, the way a carver leaves or gouges
    lino.
  - Every frame is printed like a fresh pull:
    - the paper's tooth refuses ink in a speckle that lands somewhere new with each drawing (12 a
      second);
    - the red block lands 3 px out of register, with a pixel of jitter;
    - both plates print with multiply.
  - Figures carry a cut white halo, the carver's separation line.
- **One camera for 31 winters.**
  - The house, the deodars, the fence and the snowman's spot never move. The winters change what
    is on them:
    - the sky: moon, day, overcast, dusk with a red sun, or rain;
    - the snowline on the peaks;
    - the snow on the trees and roof;
    - the ground: deep, thin, patchy, bare, or mud with puddles;
    - the roof: a TV antenna, then a dish, then a solar water heater.
  - The print gets darker as the snow goes.
- **The red block** carries warmth and family: the lit door and windows, the boy's bobble hat and
  scarf (which pass to his baby and then his daughter), the carrot, the topi's velvet band, the
  year stamp and the dusk sun.
- **The topi** is the Himachali cap. Dad gives his to the first snowman, and it goes on every
  snowman after. In 2020 it swallows one whole. In 2025 it covers the tiny one and then slides
  over his daughter's eyes.
- **Scale.** Figures stand on the ground at px/m = (y − 580) / 1.2 in the exterior; the kitchen
  is 420 px/m. Heights:
  - the boy: 1.15, 1.40 and 1.66 m, then 1.76 m as a man;
  - dad: 1.74 m;
  - his wife: 1.62 m;
  - his daughter: 1.15 m, the size he was in 1994;
  - the snowmen: 1.7, 1.4, 1.05, 0.7, 1.75, 0.45 and 0.2 m, then 8.5 cm on a steel saucer.

  The topi (0.22 m) and the carrot (0.13 m) stay the same size every year.

**Winter changes.** Each winter change is a camera flash. The screen goes to two white frames,
then one drawing with only the red block printed, then the full new print. The flashes fall on
the waltz's bars: 17.5, 20.5, 23.5, 26.5, 31.0, 34.0 and 37.0.

## Score

**Every sound is synthesised in the page; there are no recordings.**

- **Instruments.**
  - **The lead: a music box.** A tine's strong fundamental, its cantilever overtone at 6.27×
    that dies in hundredths of a second, a hint of octave, and the pluck's click.
  - **A felt piano.** Twelve slightly stretched partials, a soft hammer and the felt's thump.
  - **Also:** a plucked bass, a string pad, a glockenspiel, sleigh bells, the camera (shutter,
    flash whine, film wind-on) and a phone shutter.
  - **Foley:** snow footsteps from crushed grains, a rolling snowball, splats and flumps, a
    cork-pop carrot, a door creak, the rain, the fridge's seal and hum, a spoon scraping frost,
    steel.
- **The waltz.** It is in F at 120 to the minute in 3/4, and its bars fall on 1.0, 2.5, 4.0 and
  so on, so each winter is two bars.
  - Theme A: C–F–A | G · F | E–G–C | B♭, then home.
  - Theme B answers it.
- **How it follows the snowman.**
  - **1994:** full, theme A over the build.
  - **1998:** theme B, still full.
  - **2003:** thinner, with a tinny beat leaking from his headphones.
  - **2008:** the music box alone.
  - **2012:** everything comes back, bigger: strings, glockenspiel doubling, sleigh bells on
    every beat, a low boom on the bar.
  - **2016:** grey, minor chords and no bass.
  - **2020:** a few notes with gaps. One is cut short under the topi, and the last is left
    unresolved.
  - **2025:** the music stops for the rain.
  - **The kitchen:** the felt piano carries the theme slowly. The music box comes back note by
    note as the little snowman is built, one note per ball, eyes and nose. It stops dead under
    the topi and resumes when it lifts. While it melts, the box runs down like a spent spring,
    slower and flatter, and never finishes the phrase.
  - **The save:** a pizzicato scurry under her running feet.
  - **The window:** a glockenspiel falls as the topi slides and rises as she pushes it up. A
    B♭maj7 pad blooms as they look out.
  - **The end:** the theme once more, whole and unhurried, landing on F.
- **Measured in Firefox (`audio.mjs --twice`).** I −16.0 LUFS, LRA 9.1 LU, TP −2.0 dBTP, clipped
  0; two cold renders byte-identical (`a6ef7dae93f59f90`). The level shape follows the story:
  - the 1994 waltz at about −14;
  - 2012 the loudest at about −11;
  - the winters thinning to −19;
  - the 2025 rain at −21;
  - the kitchen at −17 to −22;
  - the end back to −16.
- **Designed quiet.** A short hush after the freezer door shuts (59.5) and a breath before the
  end theme (65.3).

## Evidence

- **`verify.mjs`:** seek is pure in t in Chromium and Firefox across the duration and every
  shot boundary.
- **Speed:** frames draw in about 11 ms (median) in Firefox, and a full render takes about two
  minutes.
- **Reviewed:**
  - the whole film on sheets, with native crops of the build, every winter and the kitchen;
  - frame strips of the slip, the throw and the first flash;
  - a silent 720p rough cut, which was sent to the user.

## Weak spots and what has not been reviewed

- **Figures:** they are side-on cut-outs. Poses read, but the motion is simple (on twos, eased
  keys). The hands are simple mitten or bare shapes.
- **Close-ups:** the kitchen inserts show objects only. Early tries at close-up hands and a giant
  face looked crude and were cut.
- **Not heard:** nobody has listened to the score yet, and I can't listen. Its meters pass, but
  the music box, the felt piano and the timing of the gags at full speed are unjudged.
