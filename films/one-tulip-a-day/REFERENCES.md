# One Tulip a Day: visual reference notes

For observation only. Do not trace. I inspected 22 photos at 800 to 1280 px, plus crops. Anything seen only on a contact sheet is marked "thumbnail".

### 1. Steam locomotive: NS 3737 (NS 3700 class, 4-6-0)
- Sources: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:HUA-151479-Afbeelding_van_de_stoomlocomotief_nr._3737_(serie_3700-3800)_van_de_N.S._op_het_emplacement_te_Roosendaal.jpg (1957 broadside, measured) · https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Utrecht_Spoorwegmuseum_Innenbereich_55.jpg · https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Spoorwegmuseum_loc_NS_3737_(2).jpg · https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Spoorwegmuseum_loc_NS_3737.JPG · https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:NS_3700_Class_No._3737.jpg · https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:NS_3737_-_Bhv.jpg (1974) · https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:HUA-163274-Afbeelding_van_de_stoomlocomotief_nr._3816_(serie_3700-3800)_van_de_N.S.jpg · specs: https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/NS_3700
- Observed (D = driving wheel, 1850 mm; 18.48 m over buffers = 10 D, which matches the photo):
  - Wheels: bogie wheels 0.5 D; three drivers at 1 D (about 20 spokes, crescent counterweights) whose tyres almost touch; tender wheels 0.65 D. The rear driver is under the cab.
  - Heights above rail: buffers 0.6 D; running board 0.85 D (drivers 1–2 rise through brass-edged splashers); boiler top 2.1 D (so driver ≈ 0.47 × boiler-top height); cab roof 2.35 D; dome 2.5 D; chimney 2.65 D.
  - Distance from the buffer face: chimney 1.6 D; bogie axles 1.0 and 2.3 D; cylinder low between the bogie wheels at 1.7 D; dome 3.2 D; drivers 3.7, 4.9 and 6.0 D; safety valves 5.15 D; cab 5.4 to 6.7 D; tender to 10 D. The connecting rod goes to the leading driver.
  - Cab: two small round-topped side windows and an oval brass number plate; the roof overhangs at the rear.
  - Front: red buffer beam with brass "No 3737"; three brass lamps (smokebox top and beam); round black smokebox door with a wheel handle and two hinge straps.
  - Livery: lined dark green; black smokebox and frames; copper chimney cap; brass dome and safety-valve casing. Rods steel (red in 1974). The tender has flat sides and flared coal plates at about cab-sill height; it had 3 axles in 1974.
- Drawing decision: Use a 10-D grid with a flat boiler line broken only by the chimney, dome, valves and cab. Brass is the spot colour.
- Not inspected: the 1957 tender's axle count; the inside cylinders.

### 2. Period carriages
- Sources: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:STAR_C147_Blokkendoos_Stadskanaal.jpg · the coaches in https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:NS_3737_-_Bhv.jpg
- Observed:
  - Blokkendoos (1925, a hauled coach from 1956): flat riveted sides, shallow arc roof, green with black waist band and roof edge, about 8 drop windows with top-lights.
  - End doors only, with tall round-cornered windows, vertical grab rails and two hanging steps. 2-axle bogies under the ends; buffers at solebar height.
  - The 1974 coaches are dark blue with a cream line, and passengers lean from the lowered windows.
- Drawing decision: Repeat one window module; doors and steps go only at the ends.
- Not inspected: compartment coaches.

### 3. Emergency brake (noodrem)
- Sources: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:HUA-152084-Afbeelding_van_een_noodrem_in_een_houten_rijtuig_van_de_N.S._(ex-Staatsspoorwegen)_te_Utrecht.jpg (1954) · thumbnail: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Notbremsgriff_um_1920.jpg
- Observed:
  - It sits high on the pier between two windows, under the cornice at luggage-rack level.
  - A cast plate with a light face, raised "NOODREM" lettering and scroll ornaments; a rod along the wall top passes through it.
  - A D-shaped stirrup handle about as wide as the plate hangs below it, with a seal wire.
  - The German example from about 1920 (thumbnail) is red.
- Drawing decision: The florist reaches up and yanks the stirrup down. The NS colour is unknown from the B/W photo.
- Not inspected: NS colours.

### 4. Conductor's signal and uniform
- Sources: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:HUA-165776-Afbeelding_van_een_perronopzichter_op_het_perron_van_het_N.S.-Utrecht_C.S._te_Utrecht,_tijdens_het_geven_van_het_vertreksein_voor_een_trein.jpg (1950s) · https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:HUA-167803-Afbeelding_van_een_conducteur_van_de_N.S._tijdens_het_geven_van_het_vertreksein_voor_een_trein.jpg (about 1978)
- Observed:
  - 1950s: a tall oval disc with a light rim and dark centre, on a stick, raised to head height at arm's length.
  - 1950s uniform: dark double-breasted jacket (2×4 buttons), tie, flat-crowned peaked cap with a cord and winged-wheel badge, whistle chain.
  - About 1978: a round "spiegelei" (white ring, dark centre) on a short handle, whistle in the mouth, shoulder satchel.
  - The photos are B/W. A search snippet quoting an Erfgoed Brabant record gives green with a white band; I did not see this myself.
- Drawing decision: Green disc with a white ring, a whistle, and the 1950s cap (not the 1970s striped one).
- Not inspected: the disc in colour.

### 5. Amsterdam Centraal train shed
- Sources: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:HUA-154576-Gezicht_op_de_perrons_onder_de_1e_perronkap_van_het_N.S.-station_Amsterdam_C.S._te_Amsterdam_met_geheel_links_een_stoomlocomotief_uit_de_serie_3700_van_de_N.S.jpg (1930s) · https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:HUA-165139-Gezicht_op_de_perronkap_van_het_H.S.M.-station_Amsterdam_C.S._te_Amsterdam,_uit_het_westen.jpg · thumbnail: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Borden_op_het_perron_van_het_Centraal_Station_links_een_bord_verwijzend_naar_he,_Bestanddeelnr_252-1060.jpg
- Observed:
  - One near-semicircular arch (span ≈ 2 × rise) covers several tracks, springing from piers at platform level.
  - Closely spaced thin lattice ribs, with glass along the crown and the flanks.
  - The glazed end screen stops at a lattice girder lettered "AMSTERDAM" about ¼ of the way up; it is open below. The ironwork is dark green-grey.
  - The platform kiosks carry white boards in black capitals ("2e PERRON WESTZIJDE") and arrow-shaped direction boards.
- Drawing decision: A semicircle with a fine rib rhythm and a pale crown strip. Name the perron instead of giving track numbers.
- Not inspected: the platform edge.

### 6. Bloemenmarkt stall
- Sources: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Bloemenmarkt_Stall.jpg (2008) · https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Bloemenmarkt_Amsterdam_2014.jpg · https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Aardig_tafereeltje_op_de_Bloemsingel_Duitse_militaire_bij_een_bloemsenstal_op_de_Bloemenmarkt_te_Amsterdam,_Bestanddeelnr_31038_084.tif (1940s)
- Observed:
  - From the canal: glass-house sheds on dark pontoons, each with a gable, making a saw-tooth roofline.
  - 2008: a scalloped striped awning, stepped crate tiers rising toward the back, and tulips upright in tapered green, black and zinc buckets, with black price tags on sticks.
  - 1940s: wooden trays, planks, clay pots and a galvanised bucket at the canal edge.
- Drawing decision: Stepped planks with zinc buckets; the awning is only confirmed for 2008.
- Not inspected: stalls in the 1950s.

### 7. Dutch bicycle with front carrier
- Sources: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:WorkCycles-Transport-GT-70cm.JPG · https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Gazelle_1954.jpg · https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Freight_bicycle_Amsterdam_NETHERLANDS_June_2008.jpg
- Observed:
  - Transportfiets: double top tubes, a tall head tube, swept-back bars with a bell by the stem, 28" wheels, and a full chaincase, mudguards and skirt guard.
  - The front rack is bolted at the fork crown and braced to the hub, so it turns with the bars.
  - The 1954 Gazelle ladies' bike has a curved second tube, a chrome headlamp and cream tyres.
  - Freight variant: a frame-fixed crate over a small front wheel.
- Drawing decision: The crate and front wheel turn together; the rider sits upright.
- Not inspected: a photo of a rider.

### 8. Tulips drying
- Sources (thumbnail): https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Withered_tulips_in_a_makeshift_vase_after_International_Women%E2%80%99s_Day_Warsaw.jpg · https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Rote_Tulpe_verbl%C3%BCht.JPG · https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Nahaufnahme_Tulpe_verbl%C3%BCht.JPG · https://www.plantsandflowersfoundationholland.org/en/drying-flowers-5-methods-tips-and-tricks/
- Observed:
  - Stems go limp and hook over, so the heads hang down.
  - The petals close into a wrinkled cup, the colour fades and the edges darken; later the petals drop.
  - Other flowers are tied with twine and hung head-down from a line or rack.
- Drawing decision: A small, closed, wrinkled cone on a thin stem.
- Not inspected: a fully dried tulip.

### 9. Amsterdam tram (GVB)
- Sources: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Amsterdamse_museumtrams_-_GVB_465_%2B_731.jpg · https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:GVB_533_(1950).jpg
- Observed:
  - Museum tram 465 is a short two-axle box: dark blue lower body, cream pillars, teak window frames and a flat 3-window front.
  - It uses a single bow collector ("beugel"), not a pantograph, with a roof number box and a yellow destination board.
  - Tram 533 (1950): rounded front, blue lower body, cream roof.
- Drawing decision: A blue box with a cream roof and a bow on top.
- Not inspected: trams in 1950s service.
